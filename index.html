When to Call a Licensed Plumber

Plumbing issues tend to get bigger the longer they wait. A planned repair or upgrade helps you keep your home running smoothly.

Consider calling DAD'S Plumbing when you are:

  • Dealing with a water heater that is not working properly.
  • Replacing an older water heater with a new one.
  • Thinking about a tankless water heater.
  • Adding a home water filtration system.
  • Noticing a leak or dripping fixture.
  • Remodeling a bathroom or replacing fixtures.

Before you call, make a note of what you are seeing and where it is happening in your home. That helps us understand your project faster.
